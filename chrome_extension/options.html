BizOS bridge settings

The extension only reads the active tab when the BizOS agent asks. It never clicks, types or sends anything from pages on its own.

Easiest: in the BizOS overlay click Connect Chrome; it fills these in for you. Manual: token is in ~/.bizos/extension-token.

Chrome may ask to let this extension access devices on your local network. Click Allow: the bridge runs on your own machine (127.0.0.1).
